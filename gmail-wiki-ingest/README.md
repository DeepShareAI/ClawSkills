# Gmail → Wiki Ingest

> ⚠️ **Requires the HiJavis iPhone app.** This skill runs inside HiJavis — install the app first, then connect Google in Settings.
> 📲 https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hijavis/id6745134765

Gmail Ingest turns the email that actually matters into pages in your personal
wiki — and leaves the rest of your inbox alone. Once a day it looks at what's
new, decides what's worth keeping, and hands you a short list to approve.

## Picture this

- A month into a project, you can't remember what you agreed with the contractor in April. It's on one page now, not scattered across forty emails.
- A friend emails you a long thought about a book. It lands on the same page as the notes you dictated about it last week.
- Your inbox is 80% receipts and newsletters. None of it reaches your wiki, and you never had to write a filter.
- Someone new starts appearing in your mail. Their name shows up on the pages they belong to, linked to everything they've touched.
- You come back from two weeks away, open the app, and the ingest that was waiting for you runs — covering the whole time you were gone.

## What it does

Every day, Gmail Ingest reads the *outside* of your recent mail — who it's from,
what the subject says, how long the thread is. It never opens a message you
haven't approved.

From that, it picks out the real correspondence: actual back-and-forth between
people. Receipts, invoices, newsletters, conference invites and platform notices
are set aside — they're the bulk of any inbox and they're not what a personal
wiki is for.

What survives shows up in HiJavis as a short review list. Tap **Confirm** and
the thread is read, distilled, and woven into your wiki — linked to the pages,
people and topics it belongs to. Tap **Discard** and it's gone, and the skill
remembers.

## How to use it

### Turn it on

Connect Google in HiJavis (one grant covers Calendar and read-only Gmail), then
enable Gmail ingest. That's the whole setup. It starts on its own from there.

### Answer the list

New candidates appear as cards you can Confirm or Discard. There's no rush —
they wait for you.

### Ask for it now

Open your HiJavis chat and say any of these:

- "ingest my email"
- "gmail ingest"
- "sync my inbox to the wiki"
- "整理邮件"
- "邮件入库"

## What makes it handy

- **It learns from your taps.** Confirm and Discard aren't just this-one-time answers — they teach it. Discard the same weekly notice a few times and it stops asking.
- **Senders you keep saying yes to stop needing a tap.** After you've confirmed someone several times, their mail goes straight into your wiki, with an undo on the card if you disagree. One discard takes that back.
- **It reads almost nothing.** Until you approve a thread, it has only seen the envelope — the sender, the subject, the date. The message itself stays on the server, and never reaches the part that does the judging.
- **It never asks twice.** A thread you've answered doesn't come back. A thread that grew new replies updates its page instead of making a second one.
- **Nothing is silent.** Every decision — yours and the automatic ones — is written down, so the wiki can always tell you where a page came from.
- **You hear from it once a day, briefly.** One line in your chat saying what the run found — including on the days it found nothing, so a quiet week never looks the same as a broken connection.

## Real-life examples

- **The long project thread:** six months of scheduling, quotes and changes become one page you can actually read.
- **The person you keep emailing:** their page fills in on its own, from the threads you confirm.
- **The 80% you don't want:** receipts and newsletters never make it to the list at all.
- **Coming back from a trip:** the run that was waiting fires when you open the app and covers the whole gap.

## Good to know

- **Confirm is the gate.** Nothing is read in full, and nothing is written to your wiki, until you say so — or until a sender has earned your standing approval by being confirmed several times, which one discard revokes.
- **Runs when you're around.** The daily check happens the next time you use the app after it comes due, not on a fixed clock. Nothing is missed — a later run just covers a longer stretch of mail.
- **The switch is in the app.** Turning Gmail ingest off in HiJavis stops it. Nothing else needs changing.
- **Read-only.** The skill can read mail; it cannot send, delete, label, or reply to anything.
