# To-dos & Spending

> ⚠️ **Requires the HiJavis iPhone app.** This skill runs inside HiJavis — install the app first, then it's ready to use.
> 📲 https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hijavis/id6745134765

To-dos & Spending keeps your list and your spending from what you say out loud. Say "I need to call the bank" or "just paid twelve dollars for lunch" while wearing your earbuds, and HiJavis turns it into a card you confirm with one tap.

## Picture this

- Walking out of a meeting you say "I need to send Mia the slides." That evening it's on your list.
- You grab a coffee and say "that was five fifty." At the end of the month you know exactly what went to Food & drink.
- You say "OK, I finally called the bank." HiJavis asks "Done?" and one tap clears it from your list.

## What it does

- **Catches your to-dos and purchases.** Only things *you* say you'll do or *you* paid for. Other people's plans and maybes are left out.
- **Asks before it counts.** Every card starts pending. Confirm keeps it, Discard drops it. Nothing counts until you confirm.
- **Spending by category.** Each purchase gets one of nine categories (Food & drink, Groceries, Transport, Shopping, Bills & subscriptions, Entertainment, Health, Travel, Other); change it on the card if it guessed wrong.
- **Never mixes currencies.** Dollars and yuan get their own totals.
- **English and Chinese.**

## How to use it

Just talk. Then open **To-dos & Spending** on the HiJavis main page, or ask in chat:

- "What's still on my to-do list?"
- "How much have I spent this month?"
- "我的待办" · "这个月花了多少"

The answers come from the same numbers the app shows. Appointments with a time ("dentist Tuesday at 3") go to your Calendar instead.
